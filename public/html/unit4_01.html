Topic vocabulary: Change
WordDefinitionExample
adaptto change your ideas or behaviour so that you can deal with a new situationA successful business is one that adapts to changing situations.
dynamiccontinuously changing, growing or developingThe world of electronics is a very dynamic area.
novelnew or unusualA TV that receives text messages is quite a novel idea.
shiftif an idea, attitude or plan shifts or someone shifts it, it changesThe plan has shifted so we'll need to have another meeting.
shift (additional meaning)a change in something, for example in someone's ideas or opinionsThere has been a real shift in attitudes on this issue.
adjustto change something slightly in order to make it better, more accurate or more effectiveYou can adjust the volume using this control here.
endureto suffer something unpleasant or difficult in a patient way over a long periodI don't know how people endure the conditions in prison.
endure (additional meaning)to last for a long timeShakespeare's works have endured for over 400 years.
persistto continue to do or say something in a determined wayYou'll get yourself into trouble if you persist in behaving in this way.
spoilto affect something in a way that makes it worse, less attractive or less enjoyableYour argument with Bill really spoiled the party.
alternateif one thing alternates with another, it happens after it and keeps being repeatedYou go first this game, and then we'll alternate.
alternate (additional meaning)happening on one day, week, etc, but not on the day, week, etc that immediately followsWe usually go to visit my grandma on alternate Sundays.
evolvewhen a type of plant or animal evolves, its physical form changes over a long period of timeDo you think people will eventually evolve into some kind of superhuman?
evolve (additional meaning)to gradually change and develop over a period of timeAs the computer industry has evolved, everything has got faster and cheaper.
potentialthe possibility to develop or achieve something in the futureEd's got a lot of potential as an athlete.
potential (additional meaning)possible or likely in the futureI think the book's a potential bestseller.
status quothe present situation or the way things usually areLet's just keep the status quo until we know what's happening.
alternativesomething that you can choose instead of something elseCan you suggest an alternative?
alternative (additional meaning)different from something else and able to be used instead of itWe're going to have to find an alternative route.
influenceto affect the way someone thinks or behaves or affect the way something happensDon't let yourself be influenced by what she says.
influence (additional meaning)the effect that a person or thing has on someone's decisions, opinions or behaviour or on the way something happensPicasso has had an enormous influence on many painters.
progressto continue to develop or move forwardYour English has really progressed this term.
progress (additional meaning)the process of developing or improvingI'm not sure that all this technological progress is necessarily a good thing.
steadyto hold something firmly without shaking or moving itCould you just steady the ladder?
steady (additional meaning)firmly held in a particular position without moving or shakingImogen gave me a steady look.
steady (additional meaning)staying at the same level, speed, value, etcPrices should remain steady over the next few months.
amendto make changes to a document, law, agreement, etc, especially in order to improve itCould you amend the report before you send it?
innovationa new idea, method, piece of equipment, etcThis innovation will mean cheap energy for everyone.
radicala radical change or way of doing something is new and very different from the usual wayThat's quite a radical suggestion.
substituteto remove one thing and put something else in its placeYou can substitute cream with milk, if you have any.
substitute (additional meaning)something that is used instead of something elseMargarine is a good substitute for butter.
conservativenot willing to accept much change, especially in the traditional values of societyI grew up in a village where most of the people were quite conservative.
innovativenew, original and advancedHis first album was innovative, but I've been disappointed by the others.
refineto make some changes to something in order to improve itYou need to refine your argument to make it clearer.
sustainto provide the conditions in which something can happen or existThe atmosphere on Mars won't sustain life.
convertto change from one system, use or method to another, or to make something do thisWe're converting to a new computer system at work, so it's chaos at the moment.
convert (additional meaning)to change your beliefs, especially your religious beliefs, or to persuade someone to do thisI was rather shocked when my dad converted to Islam.
convert (additional meaning)someone who has changed their beliefs in an important wayI didn't use to like Fiat cars, but now I've become a convert.
lastto continue existing or happening for or until a particular timeThe flight lasts five hours.
reformto improve a situation by correcting things that are wrong or unfair, or make a system work more effectivelyThe government is planning to reform the health system.
reform (additional meaning)a change intended to correct a situation that is wrong or unfair or make a system work more effectivelyThese reforms don't go far enough, in my opinion.
switchto replace one object with anotherSomeone switched their briefcase for mine when I wasn't looking.
switch (additional meaning)a change from one thing to anotherI knew the magician had changed the cards over but I didn't spot the switch.
switch (additional meaning)something such as a button or key that controls the electrical supply to a light, piece of equipment, machine, etcThis switch controls the volume.
decayto be gradually destroyed as a result of a natural process of change, or to destroy something in this wayWhen leaves decay, chemicals are returned to the soil.
decay (additional meaning)the gradual destruction of something as a result of a natural process of changeToo much sugar in our diets is a major cause of tooth decay.
maintainto make something stay the sameI find it hard to maintain my weight.
remainto continue to be in a particular situation or conditionThe interest rate is going to remain at 4%.
transformto make someone or something completely different, usually in a way that makes them more attractive, easier to use, etcJill was completely transformed by her time at university.
deteriorateto become worseThe situation in the country has deteriorated and tourists are advised not to go there.
matureto start behaving like an adult and become more sensible as you get olderAs you mature, you'll see that things are much more complicated.
mature (additional meaning)behaving in the sensible way that you would expect an adult to behaveWhy can't you be a bit more mature and stop being so childish?
reviseto change your opinion or judgment of someone or somethingI think I'm beginning to revise my opinion of Tim.
revise (additional meaning)to change, improve or make additions to something such as a book, law or piece of writingRevise your essay and I'll have a look at the changes when you've finished.
trenda gradual change or development that produces a particular resultThere's a trend towards smaller cars these days.
distortto change something such as information so that it is no longer true or accurateJournalists often distort statistics in order to tell a good story.
modifyto change something slightly, especially in order to improve it or to make it less extremeWe've modified the design to solve the problem.
revolutiona sudden or major change, especially in ideas or methodsThis invention will lead to a revolution in housework.
revolution (additional meaning)a situation in which people completely change their government or political system, usually by forceLife was quite difficult in the country following the revolution.
uniformthe same everywhereYou should find that every Hilton hotel offers a uniform standard of service.

network (additional meaning)a set of computers that are connected to each other so that each computer can send and receive information to and from the other computersThere seems to be a virus on the network.
programmersomeone whose job is to create computer programsI'd like to be a programmer and work on computer games.

onlineconnected to or available through a computer or a computer network, especially the InternetI've started an online library of songs.
online (additional meaning)on the InternetYou can find what you need online.
uploadto send documents or programs from your computer to a larger system using the InternetI'm just uploading something to my website.

Example: He took the bicycle apart to repair the brakes.

Example: We tested out the new app before releasing it.

Example: The sound of the music faded away in the distance.

Example: We used up all the milk, so I need to buy more.
